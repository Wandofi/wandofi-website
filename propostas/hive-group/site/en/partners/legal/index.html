Legal support that talks to your accounts

Many legal decisions have tax consequences. That is why the network's legal support works in coordination with your accountant.

What we do

  • Company incorporation and articles of association
  • Drafting and reviewing contracts
  • Employment matters and employment contracts
  • Corporate changes, share transfers and capital increases

Who it is for

  • Companies being incorporated
  • Companies with changes in partners or capital
  • Companies hiring staff
  • Business owners who need to review contracts with clients and suppliers

How it connects to your accounts

A share transfer, a capital increase or a new employment contract all affect the company's accounts. When the lawyer and the accountant work together, decisions are made with the full picture.

In practice, you avoid receiving two opinions that do not speak to each other.

A single point of contact

Your accountant follows the request and connects you with the specialist. You speak to someone who already knows your company.
