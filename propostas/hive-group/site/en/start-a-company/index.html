Questions about starting a company

What is the best legal structure to start with?

It depends on the activity, the number of partners, the risk and the expected turnover. We look at the options with you before anything is registered.

Can I start the company and switch accountants later?

You can, but we recommend organised accounts from the outset. It avoids corrections and ensures no deadline is missed.

How long does the whole process take?

It varies with the legal structure and the documents available. At the first meeting we present the expected timeline for your case.
