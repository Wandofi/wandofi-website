What the service includes

Hive's accounting is carried out by Certified Accountants registered with the OCC (the Portuguese Order of Certified Accountants). Every client has a named lead accountant who knows the company and is responsible for the work from start to finish.

The core service covers everything a company or a sole trader needs to stay up to date and know where they stand:

  • Bookkeeping and accounts prepared under the applicable standards.
  • Preparation and submission of tax filings, with every filing deadline met.
  • Calculation and monitoring of the company's taxes, with advance notice of amounts due.
  • Payroll and related obligations, when the company has employees.
  • Annual closing of accounts and preparation of the year-end reporting documents.
  • Replies to requests and notices from public authorities, with a clear explanation of what is at stake.
  • A quarterly review meeting with your lead accountant.

We do not work with rigid packages. We start from the diagnosis made at the free meeting and adjust the scope to the reality of your company.

Who it is for

We work with sole traders who want to stop losing time on receipts and filings, startups that need reliable numbers from day one, small and medium businesses that want to decide with up-to-date information, and established companies looking for more rigorous reporting and a contact who understands the business.

In every case the principle is the same: accounting should help you run the company, not just meet obligations.

Switching accountants without the hassle

Many companies put off switching for fear of losing documents or missing a deadline. That is why we handle the process with your previous accountant, from start to finish.

  • We meet with you to understand the current situation and anything outstanding.
  • We contact your previous accountant and request the company's accounting and tax records.
  • We confirm the filings already submitted and identify anything still due.
  • We formalise the change with the relevant authorities.
  • We agree with you the schedule for sending documents from the first month.

Throughout the handover there is always a named lead accountant, so you know exactly where the process stands. We explain the steps in more detail in the article How to switch accountants without missing deadlines.

The document portal

Every client has access to a portal to upload invoices, receipts and bank statements, check what has already been processed and receive the documents prepared by Hive. No more envelopes, lost emails or wondering whether a document arrived.

The portal also shows your company's filing calendar and any outstanding requests, so you always know what is missing and by when.

The quarterly meetings

This is what sets Hive's service apart. Four times a year, your lead accountant meets with you, in person in Amadora or online, to review the quarter.

At each meeting we look at results, expected taxes and cash flow, and prepare the decisions for the months ahead. Where it makes sense, we suggest support from a partner in the network, with the context already shared.

The result is simple: you reach year end without surprises and with decisions made in good time.

How it works day to day

After the first meeting, the work follows a predictable rhythm. During the month you upload documents to the portal, as they arrive or all at once, by the agreed date. The team records and checks the information and, if a document is missing or an expense is unclear, asks straight away rather than letting things pile up.

Before each tax filing you receive a notice of what is about to be submitted and, when tax is due, the amount and the date. There are no surprises in your bank account and no filings submitted without you knowing what they contain.

When something out of the ordinary comes up, such as a tax notice, a request from the bank or an investment decision, you speak directly to your lead accountant. You do not go through a switchboard or explain your company to someone new.

What changes compared with a traditional firm

  • A named lead accountant, instead of an anonymous team.
  • Four meetings a year included, instead of contact only at year end.
  • Advance notice of taxes and deadlines, instead of last-minute payment requests.
  • Documents kept in one portal, instead of scattered emails and paperwork.
  • Access to specialists in credit, insurance and legal who already know your company's context.

Questions about accounting

Can I switch accountants in the middle of the year?

Yes. You can switch at any time. We handle the process with your previous accountant and make sure your obligations continue without interruption.

Do I need to come to the office?

It is not required. Documents are sent through the portal and quarterly meetings can be held online. If you prefer, we welcome you in Amadora.

Who is my contact?

A Certified Accountant responsible for your company, who oversees the work and leads the quarterly meetings.

How is the price set?

Based on your legal structure, the volume of documents, the number of employees and the services included. We present the proposal after the free meeting.

Do the quarterly meetings cost extra?

No. They are included in the Hive Finance accounting service.
