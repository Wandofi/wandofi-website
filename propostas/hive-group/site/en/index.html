Frequently asked questions

How much does accounting with Hive cost?

From 250 € per month. The final amount depends on your legal structure, the volume of documents and the services you need. At the free meeting we carry out a diagnosis and present a tailored proposal, with no commitment.

How do I switch my accountant to Hive?

We handle the process with your previous accountant: we request the records, confirm what has been filed and make sure no deadline is missed during the handover.

What happens at the quarterly meetings?

We review the quarter's results, expected taxes and cash flow, and set priorities for the coming months. Meetings can be in person in Amadora or online.

Do you only work with companies in Amadora?

No. Our office is in Amadora, but we serve companies in Lisbon and across the country, in person or online.

Are your accountants certified?

Yes. The accounts are kept by Certified Accountants registered with the OCC (the Portuguese Order of Certified Accountants).

Do I have to use the partners' services?

No. Partners are an optional extra. We only suggest a specialist when it makes sense for your company.

Let us get to know your business.

A free meeting, in person in Amadora or online, to understand what you need and explain how we work. No commitment.
